Our Story

Blue Nile formed its first manufacturing outfit in 2006 under the name Blue Nile Wire Products Limited, manufacturing a wide range of wire products at Kikuyu under the brand name KIFARU.

The unit grew rapidly. In 2012, we embarked on a major diversification to manufacture hot rolled steel products under the name Blue Nile Rolling Mills Ltd. We also manufacture TMT bars branded KIFARU 500+ using Belgian technology.

Though each division is independently managed and run, they are at the same time governed by common policies and goals. In a few short years the manufacturing outfit has grown from strength to strength to become one of the leading commercial business houses in Kenya.

We believe in installing state-of-the-art technology and continually upgrading our machinery. This helps us produce high quality steel while maintaining the highest safety standards. All our products are KEBS approved.

Over the years we have developed a large regular client base which includes some of the top business houses in the country. Our staff comprise top-notch people with immense experience in technical operations, finance and marketing.
